Inspiring involvement

The habit of giving starts young.

GiveYoung.org helps young people see that generosity is not a someday idea. It is a way to learn, connect, and make a meaningful difference right now.

Our mission is to make giving and volunteering feel possible, personal, and part of everyday life.

We believe that a young people who discovers the value of showing up for others carries that perspective into their school, work, relationships, and community.
